Wind on the beam, tide under the keel, and a slip just wide enough. Gull Harbor is a boat-handling game set in a small Maine harbor, with real wind, tide and boat physics.

For iPhone and iPad

Real boat handling

Wind, tide, current and swell push every boat. Momentum, windage and prop walk decide how each hull stops, turns and backs.

A harbor career

Start in a 6.6 m RIB running water taxis, then work up through exams and licenses to pilot runs, tows, rescues and a fleet of your own.

Easy to start

Arcade handling makes the first jobs gentle. Switch to Realistic any time between jobs for the full model and 25% more pay.
