Common questions

How do I switch between Arcade and Realistic handling?

Tap Settings on the title screen, or the Arcade or Realistic button at the top of the harbor office. The handling can change any time between jobs; a job runs to the end in the handling chosen before casting off. Realistic pays 25% more, and exams are the same in both.

Is my career saved to iCloud?

Not directly. The career is saved on the device you play on, so a career doesn't carry over between devices. A device backup (to iCloud or a computer) includes the save, so restoring a backup onto a new iPhone or iPad brings the career along.

How do I start over?

Tap Reset career on the title screen. That clears your money, licenses, reputation and jobs, and can't be undone.
